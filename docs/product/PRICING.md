# Pricing Strategy

## Principle (locked)
Not a race to the bottom. Streak is free for Zerodha users — we cannot win "cheapest" and should not try.
We compete on **bundling** (1 subscription replaces 4 tools) + **realism** + **teaching AI**.

## Plans
| Plan | Price target | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | Beginners | Acquisition + funnel to paid |
| Pro | ₹99–199/mo (launch ₹99, then ₹149–199) | Intermediate | Core revenue |
| Expert | ₹299–499/mo (Phase 3) | Advanced/quant | High margin |

Annual option later (e.g., ~2 months free) to improve cash flow and retention.

## Rationale
- ₹199 × 10 = server paid; ×25 = everything covered.
- Beginner tier is FREE because beginners don't pay — but they convert to Pro as they learn. Education + AI teacher is the conversion engine.
- AI features can carry a small add-on (+₹50/mo) once usage passes the Gemini free tier.

## Payment stack (later)
- India: Razorpay (UPI/cards/netbanking) — standard.
- International (US users): Stripe/PayPal. Verify regulatory/tax needs when expanding.

## Guardrails
- No surprise paywalls for beginner-essential tools (kills trust, owner's "no customer leaves empty-handed" rule).
- Clear comparison table on pricing page (friction-free trial: 7-day Pro trial).
